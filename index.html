01

Profile

I turn drawing sets into numbers contractors can bid on. My work sits between design and construction — reading civil plans, taking off quantities, and pricing them against current material and labor markets so owners know what a project actually costs before it breaks ground.

Most of my estimates support public roadway, site utility, and structural concrete packages — from early Program of Work (POW) budgets to bid-ready Approved Budget for the Contract (ABC) estimates built on Detailed Unit Price Analysis (DUPA). I work directly with project engineers, contractors, and suppliers to keep pricing grounded in current DPWH pay items and what's actually buildable.
